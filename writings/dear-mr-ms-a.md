Dear Mr/ Ms A,

Your persistence in ignorance would have come as a surprise to me had I not been acquainted with you. Through the course of our acquaintance, you have proven yourself wholly unamenable to reason. You seem not to have even the slightest grasp of logic, for I have presented my case to you in the simplest of syllogisms, but rather than question the substance of my arguments; that is my premises and proceeding conclusions, you have resorted to retreat to fallacious argumentation, base insults and empty rhetoric.

I now find no recourse but to question not only your motives but your powers of comprehension as well. I had thought myself a great judge of character, but I failed to realize that the extent of your ability for argumentation was only fit for a conversation among the intoxicated. Should you be ready for a substantive engagement, I shall be as ready as I have repeatedly proven myself to be, but as of now, you have forced me to discontinue what has been an unfruitful and tedious engagement.

Regards,
Mwangi Rod.
Adjudged
